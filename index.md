## 🧩 What kicad-thai Includes

kicad-thai is organised around the tasks that come up again and again in Thai PCB production. Each part is designed to remove guesswork.

**Design rule presets.** These are pre-filled settings for common board types and manufacturers. Instead of typing in trace widths, clearances, and hole sizes by hand, you select a preset and let the application apply sensible values. This reduces the chance of a board being rejected by a factory for a simple rule mistake.

**Manufacturing file export.** kicad-thai helps you prepare the three files most factories ask for: Gerber files for the physical board layers, a BOM (bill of materials) listing every component, and a CPL (component placement list) telling the assembly machine where each part goes. These are the files you upload when ordering from JLCPCB, PCBWay, or a local Thai factory.

**มอก. and กสทช. checklists.** Thai products often need to meet standards set by มอก. (Thai Industrial Standards Institute) and กสทช. (National Broadcasting and Telecommunications Commission). kicad-thai includes checklists that walk you through the design-related points to review before you submit a product for certification. These checklists help you catch obvious gaps early rather than after a failed submission.

**Thai silkscreen support.** Printed circuit boards often carry labels, logos, and warnings on the top layer. kicad-thai helps you place Thai text correctly so it stays readable after manufacturing, including guidance on font size and stroke width that survive the printing process.

**Works with your AI coding assistant.** The skills in this repository are written to be used with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and GitHub Copilot. If you already use one of those tools, kicad-thai slots in alongside it. If you do not use any of them, the standalone application still gives you the presets, exports, and checklists.

## 📦 Preparing Files for JLCPCB and PCBWay

Both JLCPCB and PCBWay expect the same three core outputs. kicad-thai prepares them together so you do not forget one.

The Gerber package contains the copper layers, solder mask, silkscreen, and board outline. The BOM lists reference designators, values, footprints, and quantities. The CPL maps each component to a position and rotation on the board.

Before you upload, review the summary screen. It shows the file list, the board size, and any warnings about parts that could not be matched. Fixing a mismatch here is far cheaper than fixing it after an order is placed.

## ✅ Reviewing มอก. and กสทช. Items

The compliance checklists are organised as questions you answer yes or no. Topics include labelling, marking permanence, product documentation, and the design details that certification bodies commonly ask about.

Work through the list while your design is still flexible. If something is missing, note it and return to your design software. Keep a copy of the completed checklist with your project files, since it makes future submissions and internal reviews much faster.

## 🔤 Thai Silkscreen Guidance

Thai text has tall vowels, stacked marks, and loops that can fill in during printing. kicad-thai advises on minimum text height, stroke width, and spacing so characters stay separate after manufacturing. Follow the suggested values and preview your text at real board size before ordering.
